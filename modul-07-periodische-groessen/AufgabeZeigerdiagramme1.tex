\subsection{Pointer diagrams 1}
\Aufgabe{

Let the voltage be $\hat{U} = 325\ V \cdot \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}30^\circ}$.

\begin{itemize}
    \item [\bf a)] Draw the phasor diagram for the voltage $\hat{U}$ in the complex number space.
    \item [\bf b)] Calculate the real and imaginary parts of the complex voltage $\hat{U}$.
    \item [\bf c)] Explain the significance of the phase angle in relation to alternating voltage.
\end{itemize}
}

        \item[\bf b)] 
              The real and imaginary parts of the voltage $\hat{U}$ can be calculated using the following formulas:
              
              \begin{eqa}
                  U_{\text{Re}} &= \hat{U} \cdot \cos(\varphi) = 325 \cdot \cos(30^\circ) \approx 281,46\ V      \nonumber   \\
                  U_{\text{Im}} &= \hat{U} \cdot \sin(\varphi) = 325 \cdot \sin(30^\circ) = 162,5\ V   \nonumber
              \end{eqa}
              
        \item[\bf c)] 
                The phase angle $\varphi$ describes the time lag between the voltage and the current in an alternating current circuit.
                A positive phase angle indicates that the voltage lags behind the current (inductive load), while a negative phase angle 
                indicates that the current lags behind the voltage (capacitive load).       
    \end{itemize}
