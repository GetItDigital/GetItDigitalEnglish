\Loesung{
  The RLZ is a narrow 
  region around the pn junction in which positive ions from the n layer and negative ions from the p layer remain 
  after diffusion is complete.
  There are no free charge carriers in this zone, as the positive and negative charges neutralise each other. This creates an electric field ($\vec{E}$) that generates a drift current and suppresses the diffusion of further 
  charge carriers. The space charge zone acts as a barrier layer and prevents current flow in the reverse direction.
  See: Section \ref{sec:pn-Übergang}
