   What does the C in CMOS stand for?
   \begin{itemize}
      \item[a)] Distinction between enrichment and depletion types
      \item[b)] The use of JFETs and MOSFETs in a circuit
      \item[c)] Reflects the transfer characteristic curve
      \item[d)] Indicates the voltage sensitivity of MOSFETs
      \item[e)] Both p-channel and n-channel transistors are used in a circuit
 
   \end{itemize}
