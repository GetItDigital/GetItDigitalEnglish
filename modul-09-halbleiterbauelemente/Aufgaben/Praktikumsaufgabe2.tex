    Calculate the DC and AC voltage components across a resistor $\mathrm{R_1}$.
    
    Given:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item A mixed voltage with a DC component $\mathrm{U_{DC}} = 7{,}5\,\text{V}$
        \item Alternating voltage component $\mathrm{U_{AC}} = 2{,}3\,\text{V}$
        \item A capacitor with $\mathrm{C} = 10\,\mu\text{F}$ is connected upstream.
    \end{itemize}
    
    \begin{itemize}
        \item[a)] Calculate the total voltage without the capacitor.
        \item[b)] Describe the effect of the capacitor on the AC and DC voltage components.
    \end{itemize}
