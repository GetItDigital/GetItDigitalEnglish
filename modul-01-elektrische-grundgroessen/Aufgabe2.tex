\subsection{Electric homogeneous field 2\label{ElHomFeld2}}
\Aufgabe{
	Between the plates of the adjacent capacitor are two insulating materials with dielectric numbers $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{r1}}$ and $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{r2}}$. The capacitor is connected to a battery via
    switch $S$.\\
    \newline
    The following dimensions are given: Plate sub-areas $A_1 = A_2 = 100 \ cm^2; U_{\mathrm{B}}=100 \ V;d = 1 \ cm$.
    \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
            \item The switch is closed. Let $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{r1}}=\varepsilon_{\mathrm{r2}}=1$. How large are the capacitor charge $Q$, the electric flux density $D$, the electric field strength $E$
            and the capacitance $C_{\mathrm{AB}}$?
            \item The switch is closed. Let $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{r1}}=1, \varepsilon_{\mathrm{r2}}=3$. How has the total capacitance changed in general compared to point a)?
            The values $Q_1$ and $Q_2$, $D_1$ and $D_2$, and $E_1$ and $E_2$ must also be determined. What voltage $U_{AB}$ is established at the capacitor?
            \item In the arrangement according to point a), another dielectric is introduced \underline{after} the switch $S$ is opened, so that the following applies: $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{r1}}=1, \varepsilon_{\mathrm{r2}}=3$.
            What voltage $U_{\mathrm{AB}}$ is established at the capacitor?
    \end{enumerate}
}
