\Aufgabe{
	
An electric motor lifts a mass of m=10 kg to a height of 5 m.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item[\bf a)]
        Calculate the compressed work.
        \item[\bf b)]
        How much energy is required if the efficiency of the motor is $\eta = 80\%$?
    \end{itemize}
}
