\subsection{Electric homogeneous field 3\label{ElHomFeld 3}}
\Aufgabe{
    A capacitor for the voltage range of 100 kV is to be constructed using a dielectric in which a maximum field strength of E = 30 kV/cm is permitted.
    \newline
    The dielectric is first used in an (ideal) \underline{Plattenkondensator}.\\
        \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*1)]
        \item Sketch the arrangement.
        \item Sketch the field lines in the arrangement.
        \item Where is the location of maximum field strength?
        \item Sketch the course of the electric field strength from one capacitor plate to the other $\left| E(x) \right|$, parallel to the capacitor plates
        $\left| E_{\mathrm{Diel}}(z) \right|$ and in the capacitor plate $\left| E_{\mathrm{Pl}}(z) \right|$.
        \item Draw the 50\%- equipotential line.
        \item Calculate the minimum plate spacing $d_{\mathrm{Pl,min}}$ for the above boundary conditions.            
    \end{enumerate}
    Now the \underline{dielectric between coaxial cylinders} should be inserted.\\
    \newline
    $\left(E_{\mathrm{r}}(r)=\frac{U}{r\cdot\ln\left(\frac{a}{b}\right)};\ r_{\mathrm{a}}: Outer radius; r_{\mathrm{i}}: Radius \ of the \ inner cylinder\right)$
    \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*2)]
        \item Sketch the arrangement.
        \item Sketch the field lines.
        \item Where is the location of maximum field strength?
        \item Sketch the course of the electric field strength $E_{\mathrm{r}}(r)$ from $r=r_{\mathrm{i}}$ to $r=r_{\mathrm{a}}$.
        \item Draw the 50\%- equipotential line.
        \item Calculate the minimum outer radius $r_{\mathrm{a}}$ for $r_{\mathrm{i}} = 1;\ 2;\ 3;\ 4;\ 5;\ 10 \ cm$ and
        the corresponding thickness of the dielectric. $d_{\mathrm{i}}$.                        
    \end{enumerate}
}
